Launch an activity: wizard wireframes

Low fidelity, for discussing the flow and how the space is shared. Each frame is drawn at its real viewport size and scaled down; the dashed line is the fold. Labels are in Spanish, as a teacher sees them in es-CL. Titles, cases and courses are fictional.

fold (bottom of the viewport)numbered notes, explained under each frame
Decided on 2026-11-01, after the first review:
  • The list is ordered by last launch. A design never launched is placed by its creation date.
  • The description is required.
  • The description is shown next to the activity's name, in the dashboard's activity bar and in the activity lists, so activities launched from the same design can be told apart.
  • One switch turns off the AI services for the whole activity.
  • Step 2 checks whether the design's AI services can run before offering the switch. If they cannot, it shows today's "services not available" notice instead, before the launch.
  • The list only marks that a design uses AI. The preview names the service of each phase.

Today

A. 1920 × 1080: step 1, choose a design

  1. Stepper. Two steps, always visible: "Elegir diseño" and "Identificar y lanzar". The current step is marked, and a completed step shows a check and can be clicked to go back. It is a nav with aria-current="step".
  2. Find a design. The search filters as the teacher types, over title, case and tags, with no typeahead popup. The type chips narrow the list.
    • The list says its order: "Por último lanzamiento". The most recently launched designs come first.
    • A design never launched is placed by its creation date. A design created yesterday sits above one last launched a month ago, so a design just written for this class is at the top.
  3. One line per design, instead of a 353 px card.
    • It shows a radio button, the title, the type, the number of phases and the case.
    • "✦ IA" marks a design that uses AI services in any phase. It comes from the design itself, so the list needs no catalog of services.
    • On the right, when the design was last launched, or created if it never was.
    • The whole row selects; there are no other buttons.
    • 11 designs fit on the first screen.
  4. Designs that cannot be launched are explained, not silently hidden. Designs with errors are not listed, and the footer says how many and links to Diseños. Archived designs are not listed either.
  5. Preview of the selected design, so the teacher can check it without leaving the page.
    • It shows the type, the case, each phase with its mode and questions, and when the design was created and last launched.
    • It names the AI service of each phase with the service's own texts, as the design editor and the dashboard do.
    • "Ver diseño completo" opens the design in a new tab, so the wizard keeps its state.
  6. Action bar, fixed to the bottom. It names the chosen design and moves on with "Siguiente: identificar la actividad". The button is disabled until a design is selected. Double-clicking a row also moves on.

B. 1366 × 768: step 1, narrower

  1. Same structure, with a 380 px preview. When the type and the phases do not fit on a row's second line, the row drops them; the preview still shows them.
  2. 6 designs fit on the first screen. Today not one full card does.

C. 1366 × 768: step 2, identify and launch

  1. The chosen design stays in sight, with "Cambiar diseño" to go back to step 1. The selection, the search and the scroll position of step 1 are kept.
  2. Description, required, to recognize the activity later.
    • The label and the help say what it is for: course, section, semester or date.
    • Under it, the descriptions this teacher used in their last activities, as chips. One click fills the field.
    • "Lanzar actividad" stays disabled while the description is empty.
  3. AI services of the design, checked when step 2 opens. Here they can all run, so each one shows "Disponible", and the switch is offered, on by default. It turns the services off for this activity only, and the design does not change. A design with no AI services shows one line instead: "Este diseño no usa servicios de IA."
  4. How it will look: the activity card, with the design's title as the name and the description next to it, as the activity lists and the dashboard's bar will show them.
  5. What launching does. Launching opens the activity's dashboard, and the design becomes read-only, as today. The note says so before the launch, and that the design can be duplicated to edit it.
  6. "Lanzar actividad", the only primary action. While the launch runs, it shows "Preparando el panel…" and is disabled. If the design turns out to be invalid, the message appears in this bar.

D. 1024 × 768: step 1, one column

  1. Below 1200 px the app's side menu folds, and the stepper drops its second lines.
  2. The preview moves into the list: the selected row expands under itself, with the phases, their AI services and the case. Selecting another row collapses it.
  3. The action bar stays fixed to the bottom.

E. 1024 × 768: step 2, AI services turned off

  1. AI services turned off. The services are struck through, and a note says what changes: the phases run without the tutor and the discussion agent, and the design keeps them for later activities.
  2. In one column the activity card preview sits under the form. The action bar keeps "Atrás" and "Lanzar actividad" in sight.

F. 1366 × 768: step 2, AI services not available

  1. No switch when nothing can run. The check found that none of the design's services can run now, so there is nothing to turn off.
    • Why a service cannot run: the AI service policy does not allow it, the AI Additions connection is not set up, or the service is not installed. These are the reasons behind today's notice, which the dashboard shows after the launch; here the teacher sees it before launching.
    • If only some services can run, the switch is offered for those, and the others are listed under the same warning.
  2. The bar's summary says it too: "Servicios de IA no disponibles". The teacher can still launch; the activity runs without them, as it does today.
